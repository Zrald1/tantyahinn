All models are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 — they may
be copied, modified and used commercially without attribution (credit

Files prefixed by their kit (carSedan, person, stairs, columnSq,
columnRd, door, doorDbl, window) were baked from texture-mapped OBJs
by tool/bake_kenney.py so per-face colors live in the .mtl — the app
reads no texture maps.
- KayKit Furniture Bits (kk_* files) — Kay Lousberg, CC0 1.0
  https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Furniture-Bits-1.0
  Texture atlas colors baked per-face by tool/bake_kenney.py into the
  .mtl files so the app reads no texture maps.
